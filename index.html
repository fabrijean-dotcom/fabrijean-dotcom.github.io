Method

Three stages, in this order

I · Keyboard

At the piano

Every cue starts as an improvisation played against the picture. Theme, harmony and counterpoint are settled at the keyboard before any orchestra is involved.

II · Spotting

Shaped to the cut

The take is fitted to the scene: hits on the blows, tempo moving with the edit, space where the picture needs to breathe.

III · Realisation

Full orchestra

The piano score is realised for orchestra with AI-assisted rendering, then edited, mixed and synced to picture. The writing stays mine; the speed is a section's worth of orchestrators.

About

A pianist who writes for the screen

I am a Maltese composer and pianist. My training is classical and contrapuntal: fugue, counterpoint, the long line of the symphonic repertoire. My instinct as a writer is large-scale and dramatic.

I now bring that writing to picture. I rescore action and dramatic sequences, building each cue from a piano take and shaping it to the timing of the scene.
